Modes

Six levels from a gentle pause to a locked commitment, plus ready-made templates. Each one lists what it restricts and what it cannot do.

What no mode can do on Android: stop you from turning off Lentivault's accessibility service in system Settings, starting the phone in safe mode, or uninstalling the app. Those are protections Android gives every user. Lentivault tells you when enforcement stops, but cannot prevent it.

The levels

1 · Gentle Friction

A short pause screen before chosen apps open: a few seconds of waiting, optionally a breathing moment or your own reason for cutting back. Then you choose.

Restricts: nothing is blocked. Undo: any time. Good for: noticing automatic app opening.

Limitations: easy to tap through by design. On the web app there is no app pause at all; the extension can add a pause before chosen sites.

2 · Mindful Control

Daily allowances for chosen apps, with reminders as you approach them. Once an allowance is used, a pause screen asks for a reason before continuing.

Restricts: adds friction after the allowance. Undo: any time.

Limitations: time is counted from Android's usage data, which updates when apps move to the foreground or background, so limits can be reached a little later than the exact minute.

3 · Firm Control

Blocks chosen apps when a limit is reached or a schedule applies. A pause is possible only after a cooldown, and only a few times a day.

Restricts: blocks. Undo: change or turn off the rule in the app.

Limitations: the rule can be edited in Lentivault itself; it is a firm nudge, not a lock.

4 · Strict

A commitment for a set time (at least 15 minutes, up to 90 days per cycle). Ending early is a request that takes effect only after a delay you choose when you start (default 60 minutes, between 10 minutes and 24 hours). You can cancel the request.

Restricts: blocks chosen apps for the whole cycle. Undo: after the delay, or when the cycle ends.

Limitations: the OS-level exits listed at the top of this page.

5 · Maximum

Like Strict, with a longer delay before an early end (default 24 hours, at least 6 hours). Optionally, a code held by someone you trust ends it immediately. Minimum length 1 hour.

Restricts: blocks chosen apps. Undo: after the delay, with the trusted code, or when the cycle ends.

Limitations: the OS-level exits listed at the top of this page. Lentivault never sends the code to anyone.

6 · Ultra Extreme

A locked, timer-based block with no in-app way to end early. You choose the apps and the length (1 hour to 90 days per cycle). It can renew automatically, cycle after cycle, until you turn renewal off.

  • Renewal renews the restriction, never a payment. Lentivault is free and has nothing to pay for.
  • Explicit cutoff. Each cycle has a renewal cutoff, for example 24 hours before it ends. Turning renewal off before the cutoff ends the commitment after the current cycle. After the cutoff, the next cycle is already committed, so turning renewal off ends it after that next cycle. The app shows both clearly before you confirm.
  • Current cycle and next renewal are shown separately, with a countdown to the cutoff and reminders before it.
  • Only stronger: you can add apps while it runs, but not remove apps, shorten it, delete it, or weaken it by switching modes.
  • Emergency calls always work from the block screen, and the dialer and other essential apps are never blocked.

Limitations, stated plainly: on Android, turning off Lentivault's accessibility service in Settings, starting in safe mode, or uninstalling the app ends enforcement. Optional uninstall friction (Device Admin) only adds one step. In the browser extension, removing or disabling the extension ends it. Lentivault records when enforcement was interrupted, but cannot stop it. See the recovery guide.

Templates

Templates add ready-made rules on top of your level. Each can be previewed and changed before it is turned on.

Quick Block

Block your distracting group right now, for a few minutes or hours.

Deep Focus, Study, Work

Focus sessions and work-hour schedules that block distractions.

Sleep and Morning

Bedtime windows and a calm first hour of the day.

Social media

Allowances or schedules for social apps.

Short video

For Shorts, Reels and TikTok-style feeds. On Android the whole app is blocked, because Android does not let other apps hide one section inside an app. In the browser, the extension can hide Shorts and Reels while leaving the rest of the site usable.

Gaming, Shopping, News, Entertainment

Category-based limits for each.

Communication

Fewer interruptions from messaging during chosen hours. Calls stay available.

Digital Detox

A longer break from non-essential apps.

Daily allowance and Launch limit

A daily time budget, or a maximum number of opens per day.

Custom

Build your own from scratch.

When rules overlap

The strongest action wins (block over friction over warning). Between equal actions, the higher level wins, then a locked commitment, then the rule that ends later. The Schedules screen explains each overlap in a sentence.
