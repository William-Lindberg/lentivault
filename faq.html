Frequently asked questions

Is Lentivault really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no premium tier. "Renewal" in Lentivault means a restriction you chose starts another cycle; it never involves money.

Does Lentivault collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your device and the Android app has no internet permission. See Privacy.

Why does it need the accessibility service?

It is the only way on Android, without root or a managed device, for an app to know which app is in front and to show a block screen over it. Lentivault uses it for nothing else and stores nothing it sees.

Why is the accessibility switch greyed out?

Android 13 and newer restrict this setting for apps installed from a file. Open App info for Lentivault, tap the three-dot menu and choose "Allow restricted settings". Step-by-step guide.

Can I track my screen time without blocking anything?

Yes. Tracking only needs usage access and works with every restriction off.

Can Ultra Extreme be bypassed?

Not from inside Lentivault. On Android, the phone’s owner can always turn off the accessibility service, start in safe mode, or uninstall the app. We say this before you start instead of promising something Android does not allow. See the recovery guide.

What is the renewal cutoff?

Each commitment cycle has a deadline, for example 24 hours before it ends. Turn renewal off before the cutoff and the commitment ends after the current cycle. After the cutoff, the next cycle is already committed, so turning renewal off ends the commitment after that next cycle. The app shows the cutoff with a countdown and reminders.

Can I switch to a gentler mode during a commitment?

You can change your everyday mode, but it never touches an active commitment. Commitments can only get stronger while they run (for example by adding apps).

Can I still make emergency calls?

Always. The dialer and emergency information are never blocked, and every block screen has an Emergency call button.

Can it hide Reels or Shorts inside the Instagram or YouTube apps?

No. Android does not allow one app to hide a section inside another app, so on Android the whole app is blocked. In a desktop browser, the extension can hide Shorts, Reels and feeds while keeping the rest of the site usable.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It needs Apple’s Family Controls entitlement and a native app. The web app works in Safari for planning and timers.

Does it work with Firefox?

Yes, Firefox 128 or newer. Until a Mozilla-signed version is published, it installs as a temporary add-on, which Firefox removes on restart. See installation.

What happens if my phone restarts?

Your rules are saved on the phone. After you unlock it, Android restarts Lentivault’s service and the rules apply again. Nothing is enforced before the first unlock.

What if I change the clock?

Lentivault notices manual clock changes and keeps counting real time, so a block does not end early. Commitments are fixed to the time zone they started in.

Is Lentivault open source?

No. It is free to use but not open source; see the licence.

Does Lentivault treat phone use as an addiction?

No. It is a neutral tool that helps you use your time the way you want to. It makes no medical claims.
