Features

Everything Lentivault can do, and where it works. Platform details are on the capability page.

Tracking and reports

Today at a glance

Screen time, a goal ring, the restrictions running right now with their real state, the next scheduled block and the next renewal deadline.

Reports

Day, week, month or a custom range: per app, per category, per hour, launches, longest sessions, late-night share and comparisons with the previous period.

Neutral insights

Short, factual sentences backed by your own numbers, such as "Most of this week's use was between 21:00 and 23:00." No judgement, no scores about you.

Export

Download your history as CSV or your full data as JSON at any time. Import a backup on a new device.

Rules that adapt to you

Targets

Pick single apps, whole categories (social, short video, games, shopping, news…) or your own groups. Essential apps such as the phone dialer are never blocked.

Limits

Daily allowance, launches per day, maximum continuous use with a break, a cooldown before reopening, or an allowance that starts now.

Actions

Track only, warn, add friction (a pause, a breathing moment, a reason prompt, your own goal), or block.

Schedules

Weekly windows such as work hours or bedtime, overnight windows, skipped days and one-off exceptions. A weekly timeline shows every rule, and overlapping rules are explained: the strongest one wins and the app tells you why.

Commitments

Clear review before you start

Before a commitment begins you see every detail: which apps, start and end time, whether it renews, the renewal cutoff, how (or whether) it can end early, and what your device cannot enforce. You type COMMIT and hold a button to confirm.

Current cycle and next renewal, kept apart

The commitment screen shows the running cycle and the next one separately, with a countdown to the cutoff after which the next cycle is locked in.

Can only get stronger

While a commitment runs you can add apps, but not remove apps, shorten it or delete it. Switching modes never weakens an active commitment.

Audit trail

Every change to a commitment is recorded with its time, so you can see what happened and when.

On Android

  • A native block screen with the reason, the end time, Go home and an Emergency call button.
  • Friction screens with a countdown, breathing or a reason prompt.
  • Quick Settings tile for an instant block, and a home-screen widget with today's time and the next deadline.
  • Restrictions keep working when the app is closed and after the phone restarts.
  • Clock-change detection, so moving the clock does not end a block early.
  • Optional uninstall friction (one extra step, not a lock; see capabilities).

In the browser

  • Block sites on a schedule or for a timer, with a calm block page.
  • Per-site daily time limits.
  • Hide feeds and recommendations on YouTube (including Shorts), Instagram (Reels, Explore), Reddit, X, Facebook and LinkedIn.
  • An infinite-scroll brake that asks whether you want to keep going after a few screens.

Optional accountability

With Maximum, you can let a person you trust hold a one-time code that ends a commitment early. Lentivault stores only a fingerprint (hash) of the code, never the code itself, and nothing is sent to that person by the app: you share it yourself, if you want to.
