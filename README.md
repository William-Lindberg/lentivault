# Lentivault — Take back your time.

**Lentivault shows you where your phone time goes and stops the apps and sites you can't stop
yourself. You choose how strict: from a gentle pause up to a lock you can't undo.**

Free · No ads · No account · Your data never leaves your device

## What it does, in 30 seconds

1. **See it.** Screen time per app, per category and per hour, plus how often you open each app.
2. **Limit it.** Set daily allowances, block apps during set hours, or force a break after
   continuous scrolling.
3. **Lock it.** When willpower isn't enough, start a commitment that even you can't cancel until
   the timer runs out.

When you hit a limit, Lentivault shows a full-screen block instead of the app. Emergency calls
always stay one tap away.

## Why Lentivault

| | Most screen-time apps | Lentivault |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Subscription, or core features behind a paywall | **Free. Everything. No ads.** |
| **Account** | Sign-up required | **None** |
| **Your data** | Sent to their servers | **Stays on your phone.** The Android app doesn't even have internet permission, so it *can't* send anything |
| **Strictness** | Limits you can dismiss with one tap | **Six levels**, up to a lock that can't be dismissed |
| **Honesty** | Shows "protected" even when the system has switched it off | Shows a rule as **Active only when it's really being enforced**, and says plainly what can switch it off |
| **Browsers** | Separate app, or nothing | **Extension** that blocks sites and hides Shorts, Reels and endless feeds |

## Pick how strict you want to be

| Mode | What it feels like |
|---|---|
| **Gentle Friction** | A short pause before a distracting app opens. A nudge, not a wall. |
| **Mindful Control** | A daily budget and a breather before you reopen an app. |
| **Firm Control** | A hard daily allowance, and distracting apps are blocked at night. |
| **Strict** | A timed commitment you can only end after a waiting delay, so cravings pass first. |
| **Maximum** | A long delay, and optionally a trusted person who has to approve ending early. |
| **Ultra Extreme** | No early exit at all. A locked commitment that can **renew itself automatically**, so the lock comes back next cycle too. You see exactly when you can still opt out. Renewal renews the *restriction* only; it never costs money. |

Also built in: **Quick Block** from the notification shade, and ready-made **Deep Focus, Study,
Work and Sleep** presets.

## 13 timers, all of which actually block

Countdown blocks, *until a time*, *until bedtime*, *until tomorrow*, *until a date*, focus sessions,
Pomodoro, daily allowances, continuous-use limits, cooldowns before reopening and more. They don't
just count down: they enforce.

## Where it works

| Platform | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Android 8+** | Tracking, app blocking, all modes and timers, schedules, goals, reports (CSV/JSON export), home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile |
| **Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Firefox** | Site blocking, time limits per site, schedules, hides YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and home feeds, scroll brake |
| **Web app** | Planning, focus timers, reports. A browser can't watch other apps, and the web app tells you so. |

iPhone isn't supported yet, because it needs special permission from Apple.

## Install

**Android**
1. Download `lentivault.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/William-Lindberg/lentivault/releases/latest)
   and open it. Allow installing from your browser if Android asks.
2. Follow the setup inside the app. It asks for **Usage access** (to see your screen time) and
   **Accessibility** (to show the block screen).
3. On Android 13 and newer, if the Accessibility switch is greyed out, go to App info → ⋮ →
   **Allow restricted settings**, then try again. This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.

**Browser extension**
- **Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera:** download the Chromium zip and unzip it. Open
  `chrome://extensions`, turn on *Developer mode*, then click *Load unpacked* and select the folder.
- **Firefox:** download the Firefox zip. Open `about:debugging` → *This Firefox* → *Load Temporary
  Add-on* and select the zip. It stays loaded until Firefox restarts, until the signed version is out.

## Good to know

- **Version 0.1.0 is a test release.** It passes all its automated tests but hasn't been tested on
  many real phones yet. Found a problem? Please open an [issue](https://github.com/William-Lindberg/lentivault/issues).
- **No app can fully lock a phone you own.** On Android you can still turn off the Accessibility
  service, start in safe mode or uninstall the app, and that ends blocking, even in Ultra Extreme.
  Lentivault tells you this before you commit and records when it happens.
