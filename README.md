# Lentivault — Take back your time.

Free, private screen-time tracking and anti-scrolling for Android and desktop browsers.
No ads, no accounts, no analytics. Your data never leaves your device (the Android app does not
even have the internet permission).

**Website & download:** https://william-lindberg.github.io/lentivault/

## Download

- **Android app (APK):** from the [latest release](https://github.com/William-Lindberg/lentivault/releases/latest)
  or the [download page](https://william-lindberg.github.io/lentivault/download.html).
- **Browser extension** for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Firefox: zip files on the same release.
- **Web app:** https://william-lindberg.github.io/lentivault/app/ (planning, focus timers and demo
  data; a browser cannot track or block other apps, and the web app says so).

Version 0.1.0 is a **test release**: it builds and passes its automated tests, but it has not yet
been tested on real phones. Please report problems through the Issues tab.

## What it does

- Tracks screen time per app, per category and per hour (Android, with Usage access).
- Six modes from Gentle Friction to **Ultra Extreme**, a locked commitment timer with optional
  automatic renewal and a clearly displayed opt-out cutoff. Renewal renews a restriction. It is
  never a payment.
- Timer Center with 13 timer types: countdown blocks, focus, Pomodoro, daily allowances,
  continuous use, cooldowns, bedtime and more.
- Schedules, goals, reports with CSV/JSON export, a home-screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.
- Emergency calls always stay available.
