Take back your time.

Lentivault shows where your screen time goes and helps you set the limits you choose: from a gentle pause before opening an app to a locked, timer-based commitment. Free, private, and honest about what each device can enforce.

No account. No ads. No analytics. Nothing leaves your device.

What it does

See your time clearly

Daily and weekly screen time per app and category, launches, longest sessions and late-night use. Tracking works even when every restriction is off.

Add a pause, a limit or a block

Choose apps, categories or groups. Set daily allowances, launch limits, continuous-use breaks, schedules or a short pause screen before an app opens.

Hide endless feeds in the browser

The extension blocks sites on your schedule and can hide Shorts, Reels, home feeds and recommendations, with a gentle brake on infinite scrolling.

Commit when you want to

Strict, Maximum and Ultra Extreme turn a decision into a timer-based commitment that the app will not let you weaken while it runs.

Modes, from gentle to locked

Start light and move up only if you want to. Every mode explains what it restricts, how hard it is to undo, and what your device cannot enforce. Compare all modes.

  1. 1Gentle Friction

    A short pause before a chosen app opens. Continue whenever you like.

  2. 2Mindful Control

    Daily allowances with calm reminders and a reason prompt once they are used.

  3. 3Firm Control

    Blocks when a limit or schedule applies. Pausing is possible after a cooldown, a few times a day.

  4. 4Strict

    A commitment for a set time. Ending early is a request that takes effect only after a delay.

  5. 5Maximum

    A longer delay before an early end, optionally with a code held by a person you trust.

  6. 6Ultra Extreme

    No in-app way to end early. Timer-based cycles, optional renewal with a clear cutoff.

The Timer Center

Thirteen timer types that really drive the rules, not just a countdown on screen: Anti-Scroll, App usage, Countdown block, Deep Focus, Break, Daily limit, Cooldown, App launch, Bedtime, Pomodoro, Continuous use, Custom and Ultra commitment. Start one in two taps from presets such as 15 minutes, 1 hour, until bedtime or until tomorrow morning.

Honest about limits

Lentivault never shows a restriction as active unless the device has confirmed it is enforcing it. On Android you can always turn off its accessibility service or uninstall it in system Settings; we say so up front instead of pretending otherwise. Emergency calls always stay reachable from the block screen.

Private by design

On your device only

Usage history, rules and commitments are stored on your phone or in your browser. There is no Lentivault server.

No internet permission

The Android app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Free forever

No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, no tracking. Renewal in Lentivault only ever renews a restriction you chose, never a payment.
